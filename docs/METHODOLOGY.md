# NutriPlay Scientific Methodology

## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation

Athletes in the Indian subcontinent face unique physiological and sociocultural nutritional challenges:
- High reliance on carbohydrate-dense vegetarian or Jain dietary patterns with limited traditional knowledge of plant-protein optimization.
- Environmental heat stress with high humidity across tropical plains and coastal metros, leading to rapid electrolyte depletion and hyponatremia risk.
- High economic variability in food purchasing capacity among grassroots and collegiate athletes.
- High prevalence of Low Energy Availability (LEA) and Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S), particularly among female athletes and weight-category sports.

Existing mobile applications are typically Western-centric, reliant on Western databases, ignorant of Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT), and lack biometeorological environmental adjustments.

## 2. Research Objectives

1. Develop a software-only, transparent decision-support system tailored to Indian athletic demographics.
2. Formulate a multi-objective optimization framework balancing nutritional adequacy, cultural dietary compliance, and daily food budget constraints.
3. Integrate biometeorological heat index calculations (NOAA Rothfusz regression) to dynamically modulate fluid and electrolyte guidance.
4. Establish an ethical, non-diagnostic RED-S screening mechanism grounded in IOC consensus guidelines.
5. Provide explainable mathematical rationales for every recommendation to support sports science education and viva defense.

## 3. Data Grounding & Ethics

- Nutritional composition is grounded in **IFCT 2017 (Indian Food Composition Tables)** published by the National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR-NIN), Hyderabad.
- Micronutrient baselines (Iron: 19mg male, 29mg female; Calcium: 1000mg; Sodium: 2000mg) are grounded in **ICMR-NIN 2020 RDA guidelines for Indians**.
- Exercise expenditures use the standardized **Compendium of Physical Activities** and position-specific MET adaptations.
- Market procurement costs reflect authentic Indian domestic indices (2024–2026).
- All synthetic cohort experiments utilize verified realistic athlete ranges.
